    About the data, KPI definitions and assumptions
    Total complaints
    Count of complaint records matching the current filters.
    Timely response rate
    Records with Timely response = Yes ÷ all records in view.
    Consumer disputed rate
    Records with Consumer disputed = Yes ÷ all records in view (open cases included).
    Relief rate (closed)
    Closed complaints whose response was “Closed with monetary relief” or “Closed with non-monetary relief” ÷ closed complaints in view.
    In progress
    Records with Status = In progress (company response “In progress”), and their share of the view.
    Top issue
    Most frequent Issue in view.
    • Each chart ignores its own selection when counting, so alternatives stay visible; every other panel and KPI honours it.
    • Request type is read as the channel by which the complaint was received (Web, Phone, Referral…); “Referral” is kept as supplied.
    • Consumer disputed is read as the consumer disputing the company’s response; it is populated for in-progress cases too, so they stay in the denominator.
    • 2011 starts in August and the last record is in December 2020, so those two years are partial.
    • The state map uses simplified hand-drawn outlines (no external geodata); the ACT is shown as a call-out circle.
    • Complaint ID, Sub-product and Postcode are embedded but not charted; Postcode is not used for the map because States are supplied.
